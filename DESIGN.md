## Brand & Style
The design system bridges the precision of modern restorative dentistry with the bespoke, reassuring warmth of high-end concierge care. Inspired by the brand mark’s fluid heart silhouette embracing a joyful family unit, the aesthetic projects clinical authority, generational trust, and serene luxury.

### Visual Movement & Tone
- **Movement:** Modern Minimalist meets Warm Tactile Luxury. It avoids the cold, sterile blues common to clinical portals in favor of restorative linen whites, deep charcoal-espresso structure, and luminous warm champagne gold accents.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Calming, prestigious, reassuring, and impeccably clean. Patients should feel treated as esteemed guests within an architectural sanctuary rather than clinical subjects.
- **Target Audience:** Multi-generational families seeking top-tier aesthetic and restorative dental services, value-driven patients seeking comfort and state-of-the-art dental wellness.

## Colors
The palette extracts direct harmony from the brand's identity: deep clinical charcoal anchoring the composition, balanced against refined champagne gold, luminous gilded highlights, and warm organic linen.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#1E2124`):** Deep Clinical Charcoal. Used for primary typography, impactful CTA surfaces, structural headers, dark hero containers, and high-contrast iconography.
- **Secondary (`#C9A86A`):** Warm Golden Bronze / Champagne. Applied to primary button fills, prominent interactive states, badge backgrounds, and secondary focus indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Soft Metallic Gold. Reserved for subtle gilded borders, active star ratings, micro-accent badges, and active state highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Rich Warm Linen / Ivory. The primary ambient background, ensuring zero ocular fatigue and eliminating sterile clinical white while retaining a pristine, hygienic clarity.
- **Surface Layering:** Pure Clinical White (`#FFFFFF`) serves as elevated cards and interactive container backgrounds to pop cleanly off the warm ivory backdrop. Muted borders utilize a delicate warm sand tone (`#E8E3DA`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision softened with open apertures and friendly curves, perfectly balancing modern European medical design with high-touch hospitality. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Styling
- **Headlines:** Use tightened negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on larger sizes with bold (`700`) and semi-bold (`600`) weights to produce clean, editorial, and commanding statement headlines.
- **Body:** Set at normal letter tracking with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to ensure effortless legibility across clinical treatment descriptions, consent forms, and patient charts.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in semi-bold and bold with intentional positive letter-spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.06em`), frequently deployed in uppercase for metadata categories (e.g., "PREVENTATIVE CARE", "PEDIATRIC SUITE").

## Layout & Spacing
The design system operates on an 8-point harmonic grid system designed around air, openness, and uncluttered spatial breathing room. 

### Spatial Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column responsive grid with a 1280px max-width container, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and minimum 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** An 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** A 4-column fluid system with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are partitioned with deliberate, expansive vertical gaps (`space-xl` multiplied by 2 or 3 for section breaks) reflecting high-end architectural clinic spaces.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through subtle layering, ambient warm shadows, and whisper-thin hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#FAF8F5` surface.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Clinical Blocks):** Elevated on `#FFFFFF` with a dual-layer warm tint shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.08)` and bordered by a crisp `1px solid #ECE7DE`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Dropdowns & Modals):** Crisp white panels with `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.20)`.
- **Dark Mode / Contrast Accent Sections:** Deep charcoal panels (`#1E2124`) featuring soft inward gold glows or faint hairline borders of `rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.25)` to simulate warm clinical ambient lighting.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors the organic contour of the brand's heart ribbon and familial gestures. 

### Shape Parameters
- **Standard UI Elements (`roundedness: 2` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Applied to form fields, treatment card containers, table headers, and calendar booking blocks.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px):** Applied to status chips, action badges, and primary interactive CTA buttons, conveying gentle, protective, and humanized touchpoints without aggressive sharpness.
- **Feature Surfaces (`rounded-lg` 1rem / `rounded-xl` 1.5rem):** Applied to major patient dashboard modules, testimonial carousels, and image containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Champagne Gold):** Pill-shaped with `#C9A86A` background, `#1E2124` text, bold `label-lg` typography, and subtle micro-transition shifting to `#BFA15F` on hover with a warm gilded glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Authoritative (Charcoal):** Pill-shaped with `#1E2124` background and `#FAF8F5` text. Hover shifts to `#2A2E33`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Pill-shaped with 1.5px border in `#C9A86A`, transparent fill, and `#1E2124` text; fills with `rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.10)` on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2DDD5`, height 48px, padded with `space-md` horizontally, rounded to `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#C9A86A` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.15)`.
- **Labels:** Floating or top-aligned `label-md` in deep charcoal `#1E2124` with placeholder text in soft warm slate (`#7C7E82`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Specialization Tags:** Pill-shaped with internal padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. 
- **Style Variants:** 
  - Standard tags utilize `#FAF8F5` background with `1px solid #ECE7DE` and `#1E2124` label text.
  - Active/Featured tags utilize soft champagne fill (`rgba(201, 168, 106, 0.15)`) with `#8A6B29` text.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Treatment & Doctor Cards:** Built on clean white `#FFFFFF` foundations with rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), subtle warm sand borders (`#E8E3DA`), and ambient warm shadow. 
- **Hover Transitions:** Card elevates with `-4px` translation and receives an intensified warm rim highlight (`border-color: #C9A86A`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** 20px width/height, rounded corners (circle for radio, 4px rounded for checkbox), `1.5px solid #C9A86A`. Active states fill with `#1E2124` displaying a crisp champagne gold checkmark or inner pip.

### Specialized Clinic Components
- **Appointment Time Slates:** Outlined pill chips with dynamic states (available, selected, reserved) utilizing champagne gold highlights for instant clarity.
- **Family Profile Switcher:** Segmented controls with warm linen toggles and smooth rounded switches to switch between family member dental records seamlessly.
